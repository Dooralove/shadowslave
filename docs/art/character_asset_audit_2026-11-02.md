# Character/enemy asset audit — 2026-11-02

**Method:** read-only GLB JSON/accessor parse + Godot/Blender inspection.  
**Scope:** characters and monsters created or sourced during the current rework.  
**Companion:** `docs/art/generation_queue_2026-11-02.md` (what to generate next).

## 1. Key findings

1. **The main cast is not a missing-asset problem; it is a replacement problem.** All P1 assets exist, but they are MPFB bodies with low-poly clothing, very high primitive counts, no tangents, and every material is double-sided. The visual/retarget problems are visible in `docs/art/previews/p1_characters/`.
2. **P1 cast draw calls are high for the polygon count.** Hero 23 primitives / 9 materials, Veteran 23/9, Scholar 20/8, police 25/11, v2 gear up to 86/12. Materials are mostly unique per asset, so joining by material cannot collapse much; the real fix is new meshes, not just merging.
3. **No P1 asset exports tangents.** Normal maps are present on skin/clothes/shoes, but without MikkTSpace tangents the normal mapping is unreliable in Godot.
4. **All P1 materials are double-sided.** This hides flipped-normal errors but doubles overdraw; after the new models are rigged, set opaque closed meshes to single-sided.
5. **Mountain King p3 is geometry-heavy and textureless.** 75,643 tri across three LODs, 18 primitives, 6 materials, zero UVs, zero textures, zero tangents. The new generated model is the better path; p3 remains fallback.
6. **Carapace Scavenger was textureless and had no UVs on most parts.** It was 9,700 tri / 15 primitives / 5 materials / all double-sided. A v2 texture/UV/tangent pass is being prepared; the generation queue still asks for a higher-quality sculpt.
7. **Larva is the healthiest existing monster asset,** but still heavy: 49,508 tri across 3 LODs, one 2048² albedo PNG, 10.2 MB GLB. Texture resize to 1024/512 is the next safe optimization; the model itself is acceptable.
8. **Found assets are not automatically final.** The Stone Knight statue is a found source, not a canon statue; it needs adaptation/optimization. Balthazar/other Sketchfab humans were evaluated only as fallbacks, not integrated.
9. **Nephis, Cassia and Echo are markers, not implemented characters.** Do not generate them until their scene/gameplay is defined.
10. **Coral and Soul Sea kits are built but not integrated.** The missing work there is integration/LOD/material setup, not new generation.

## Notes

- `UV0`/`UV1`/`Tangents`/`Colors` count mesh primitives that have the attribute, not vertices.
- `Double-sided` counts materials with `doubleSided=true`; high counts mean overdraw and possible flipped-normal masking.
- Dimensions are local POSITION accessor bounds, ignoring node transforms and LOD visibility.
- Texture memory: an embedded 20482 PNG can cost ~22 MB VRAM after upload; inspect the per-asset report for exact values.

## 3. Practical consequences

- **Do not generate mass crowd first.** It multiplies asset count without fixing the main-cast problem.
- **Generate P0/P1 characters and monsters first**, then optimize, rig and test; keep all current GLBs as fallback.
- **For P1 replacements:** use A-pose, no base, no weapon in hand, fitted clothing; then retarget to the 53-bone rig and bake/validate UAL.
- **For monsters:** generate neutral pose, then build a simple game rig (or bind to existing AI) and author 5 clips (idle/walk/telegraph/attack/death).
- **For all accepted assets:** export tangents, set opaque closed meshes single-sided, resize textures, add LOD1/2, verify in Godot.
